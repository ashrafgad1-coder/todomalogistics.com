Fleet Expansion Support

Fleet Expansion Support is an operational framework focused on supporting organizations in planning for fleet expansion and coordinating the activities of that expansion within a defined scope of work, in line with the approved procedures and operating requirements. The service is designed with a clearly defined scope of responsibilities, addressed in detail in the following sections.

Scope of responsibility

Fleet Expansion Support focuses on a set of operational responsibility areas related to the fleet-expansion stage, and includes:

Planning fleet expansion

Coordinating fleet expansion

Operational-capacity readiness for expansion

Aligning fleet expansion with operating requirements

Adherence to the operational procedures related to expansion

Operational activities

The operational activities within Fleet Expansion Support include the following:

Planning fleet expansion

  • Assessing fleet-expansion requirements according to the project's scope and operating requirements.
  • Preparing an operational plan for fleet expansion in line with the project's objectives.

Coordinating fleet expansion

  • Arranging the stages of fleet expansion according to the timeline execution priority.
  • Defining the operational connection points needed between the successive expansion stages.

Operational-capacity readiness for expansion

  • Identifying the gap between the current operational capacity and the target operational capacity after expansion.
  • Preparing the operational arrangements needed to absorb the planned increase in operational scope.

Aligning fleet expansion with operating requirements

  • Reviewing the fleet-expansion plan in light of the approved operational priorities.
  • Adjusting elements of the expansion plan when a conflict arises with existing operating requirements.

Adherence to the operational procedures related to expansion

  • Applying the approved operational procedures specific to fleet expansion.
  • Verifying adherence to the operational procedures during the execution of the expansion stage.

How the service is delivered

Fleet Expansion Support is delivered through an operational methodology that begins by assessing fleet-expansion requirements according to the project's scope and operating requirements, then preparing an operational plan for fleet expansion, followed by arranging the stages of fleet expansion according to the timeline execution priority and defining the operational connection points needed between the successive expansion stages.

The service continues by identifying the gap between the current operational capacity and the target operational capacity after expansion, then preparing the operational arrangements needed to absorb the planned increase in operational scope, followed by reviewing the fleet-expansion plan in light of the approved operational priorities and adjusting elements of the expansion plan when a conflict arises with existing operating requirements — while applying the approved operational procedures specific to fleet expansion and verifying adherence to the operational procedures during the execution of the expansion stage, until the scope of the service is complete.

Service boundaries

Fleet Expansion Support is limited to the activities set out within its scope of responsibilities, and does not extend to operational responsibilities outside this framework.

This service does not include: fleet operations management, recruitment, staffing, human-resources management, or technical vehicle maintenance.

When executing projects that involve multiple operational services, the scope of Fleet Expansion Support is managed independently, with the responsibilities of each service remaining within their defined boundaries.
