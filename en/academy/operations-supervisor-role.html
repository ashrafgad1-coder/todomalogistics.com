The role of the operations supervisor

✍️ TODOMA Editorial Team⏱ 5 min read

The operations supervisor is the link between the delivery captains and the daily operation. Their role isn't just "monitoring", but organizing, following up, and leading in a way that makes the work run smoothly. This article is for anyone working as a supervisor or aspiring to be one.

From execution to supervision: what changes?

Many supervisors were outstanding delivery captains. But moving into supervision is a shift in mindset, not just a promotion in title:

  • From "my task" to "the team's flow": you're no longer responsible for your own orders, but for the smooth running of a group.
  • From execution to enabling: your success is now measured by your team's performance, not your individual performance.
  • New skills you build: clear communication, fairness in decisions, and calm under pressure.

A tip for anyone who's just started: your first priority is to get to know your team — who's capable, and who needs support — before you start changing anything.

The core responsibilities

The supervisor follows up on the distribution of work, makes sure everything is running on time, and steps in at any disruption. In short: they see the big picture while each captain focuses on their task.

Following up on captains supportively

Good follow-up is supportive, not pressuring:

  • Know who needs help before the problem grows.
  • Give guidance clearly and respectfully, not in front of customers or the rest of the team.
  • Build trust, because a captain who trusts their supervisor reports a problem early instead of hiding it.

Making a quick, fair decision

A large part of the supervisor's work is dealing with situations escalated to them (like those in "Order problems and returns").

A practical example: a captain escalates a dispute with a customer over an order. A good supervisor listens to clear information from both sides as much as possible, decides quickly and fairly, and documents what's needed, so the captain returns to work without a long disruption. Speed with fairness is the required balance.

Leading by example

The team is influenced by the way their supervisor acts more than by their words. A disciplined, calm supervisor usually creates a team that resembles them.

This article is about supervision and leadership; for the details of coordination between teams, see "The basics of operational coordination".
