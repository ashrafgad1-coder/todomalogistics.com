Services designed to meet the operational needs of logistics businesses

TODOMA Logistics brings together a set of operational services that support business needs across different areas of logistics operations. This page offers an overview of these services, as a starting point before moving to the dedicated pages that present the details of each service on its own.

Fleet Operations Management

Managing the day-to-day operations of transport fleets by organizing operational activities and supporting continuity of performance.

Managed Delivery Operations

Coordinating delivery operations to ensure a steady flow of work and follow-up on the execution of operational tasks.

Field Operational Support

Companies' operational needs differ according to the nature of their current operations. Recognizing where these operations stand helps identify the right starting point, before moving to the detailed page of the related service.

Some operations may benefit from more than one service at the same time, depending on the nature of the activity and its operational needs. Each service keeps its independent scope, whether used on its own or as part of a set of integrated services.

Some operations may benefit from combining Fleet Operations Management and Managed Delivery Operations when they require managing the fleet and coordinating delivery operations at the same time.

Some clients may combine Fleet Operations Management and Fleet Expansion Support when planning to expand the fleet's scope while keeping existing operations steady.

Some operations may call for combining Field Operational Support and Performance Monitoring & Reporting to support field execution teams while tracking performance indicators at the same time.

The details of each service can be found on its dedicated page, to understand its scope and identify the most suitable combination when needed.
